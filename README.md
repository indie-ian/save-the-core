# Godot Wild Jam #99 Submission

The Earth's core is shutting down. Race to the core to jump-start it back to its functioning state. **How fast can you save the world?**

---

## Controls

* **Esc** or **P**: Pause the game
* **Mouse**: Steer
* **Left-Click**: Charge your drill
* **Right-Click**: Move slowly *(Tip: Use this to cancel a charge before colliding with a wall)*

---

## Gameplay & Mechanics

* **Damage**: Occurs when you collide with walls.
* **Overheat**: Your temperature rises in two ways:
  * **Top Gauge (Engine)**: Overcharging your drill.
  * **Bottom Gauge (Drill)**: Digging or hitting a wall.
* **Liquid Bar**: Used to cool either the engine or the drill. Hover over the switch with your mouse and press the **A** button to activate it.
